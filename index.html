About

Research at the interface of quantum information, thermodynamics, dynamics and quantum hardware.

My research lies in quantum information science, with emphasis on the thermodynamic and statistical analysis of information processing, quantum resource theories, Markovian and non-Markovian dynamics, quantum device engineering, and cavity quantum electrodynamics.

I joined IIIT Hyderabad in December 2019. My research program combines fundamental quantum information theory with experimentally motivated quantum hardware, cavity-QED systems, quantum thermo-electronics and quantum opto-mechanics.

Research

A connected research program spanning fundamental theory, quantum dynamics and device-oriented quantum science.

01 · Devices

Quantum Thermo-Electric Circuits

Quantum thermal analogues of electric circuits, heat-current driven architectures, thermal circuit components and steady-state quantum device engineering.

02 · Hardware

Quantum Opto-Mechanics & Cavity QED

Quantum devices motivated by cavity-QED and atom–mirror interaction platforms, including thermodynamic cycles and experimentally relevant device models.

03 · Dynamics

Quantum Dynamics & Resource Theories

Non-Markovianity, ergodic channels, quantum activity, entanglement, coherence, information backflow and resource-theoretic descriptions of quantum dynamics.

04 · Open systems

Open Quantum Systems

Microscopic system–environment dynamics involving spin-boson, fermionic and finite-bath models, including approaches beyond standard Born–Markov restrictions.

05 · Information

Quantum Information Processing

Quantum switches, quantum channels, noise mitigation, entanglement and information-theoretic structures relevant to computation and communication.

06 · Metrology

Quantum Metrology

Metrological approaches for detecting unknown interaction Hamiltonians and probing complex quantum systems through circuit-inspired architectures.
